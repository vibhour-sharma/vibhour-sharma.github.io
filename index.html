01 / THE PROBLEMDISCOVER → FRAME

THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

One employee change.
Too many ways to go wrong.

Every hire, transfer or promotion could change an employee's financial costing context. But an HR selection wasn't automatically a valid Finance or ERP selection.

The challenge was not automating a dropdown—it was keeping a decision accurate from its first click to financial posting.

⌁
FRAGMENTED STRUCTURE

Two hierarchies, one employee

Workday worker attributes and Finance master data could describe the same organization differently, creating ambiguous or invalid costing options.

03 / PRODUCT THESISPRINCIPLES → ROADMAP

THE STRATEGY

Prevent early.
Explain precisely.
Govern centrally.

We approached the challenge as two complementary workflows: guide a valid choice before it becomes an accounting problem, then make deviations detectable and correctable.

Design principleAutomation should increase trust—not hide uncertainty.
01

PREVENT

Manager guidance sourced from approved finance mappings—not free-form interpretation.

02

DERIVE

Consistent ERP-aware costing strings with explicit field ownership and rule validation.

03

REMEDIATE

Transparent exception categories, human approvals, routing and audit history.

04

OBSERVE

Quality reporting that connects a metric to an owner and a next action.

DOCUMENTED 2025 ROLLOUT PLAN · NOT RELEASE CONFIRMATION

Change the behavior first.
Change the financial string second.

12 / PM LEARNINGSTRANSFERABLE PRODUCT JUDGMENT

TAKEAWAYS

What the enterprise
taught me about product.

Product quality in enterprise systems lives in the unglamorous places: ownership, state transitions, clear exceptions and valid definitions.

01

Master-data design is product design.

When the valid options are wrong, UI polish cannot rescue a workflow. Governance shapes the experience.

02

Edge cases define trust.

Empty, ambiguous, stale and inconsistent mappings should have designed responses, not silent defaults.

03

Phases reduce dependency shock.

Guided behavior can improve before every payroll, ERP and finance downstream dependency is ready.

04

Tests encode business truth.

Acceptance cases make organizational relationships and financial assumptions precise and reviewable.

05

Resolution beats a green dashboard.

A data-quality indicator matters when it leads the right person toward a controlled corrective action.

WHY IT TRANSFERS

This work strengthened the product muscles required in technical and platform PM roles: simplifying high-stakes decisions, understanding customers inside complex organizations, designing safe operational journeys and translating ambiguous requirements into measurable, testable system behavior.
